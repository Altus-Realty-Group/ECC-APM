# ECC folder plan - matching Assets Under Management
Updated November 11, 2026. This replaces the earlier expanded folder proposal.

## Layout
Base: /Altus App Folder/ECC/Production/Assets Under Management

For each ownership entity:
- <Ownership name>
  - <Property address>
    - 1.  Tenant Documents
    - 2.  Property Pics & Video
    - 3.  Improvements & Construction
    - 4.  Legal
    - 5.  Insurance

The five property folder names above match the existing asset templates.
Use the existing ownership and property names after verifying the relationship.
Do not add UUIDs, document-category trees, or lifecycle folders to this visible
hierarchy. ECC keeps record identities, permissions, versions, matter links and
delivery receipts in the database behind the familiar folder structure.

Legal files are organized in the property's existing 4.  Legal section.
Do not invent additional court, judgment or packet folder trees by default.
ECC can group a matter's related documents in its dashboard.

## Handling existing files
The original Assets Under Management and legal folders stay intact.
Intake is COPY ONLY. Preserve source provenance. Do not silently merge repeated
addresses or ownership labels, overwrite collisions, move originals, or delete
source files. Destination access must be verified before confidential copies.
